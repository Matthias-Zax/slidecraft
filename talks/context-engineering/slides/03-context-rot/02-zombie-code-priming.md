[STAGE: Tell as a story with expressive timing]
Imagine you are the head chef at a high-end restaurant.
A ticket comes in: "Table 4, medium rare steak. SEVERE PEANUT ALLERGY."
If that is the only ticket on your prep board, you are laser-focused. You sterilize the knives, check the oil, execute safely.
Now imagine your prep board has 200 receipts from yesterday, old napkins, coffee stains, and customer complaints from last Tuesday.
The peanut allergy note is still there—at position 42.
What happens during dinner rush? You miss it.
When you leave an IDE chat open for 3 hours, you are forcing the model to work on that messy prep counter.
`Ctrl+L` or starting a fresh chat is not losing progress—it is wiping the counter clean.

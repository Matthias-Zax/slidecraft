[STAGE: Point to the equation on the screen]
Here is the core of every modern Large Language Model: Vaswani et al., 2017.
Scaled Dot-Product Attention.
You have three matrices:
Q is the Query: what the model is generating right now.
K is the Key: the address tag of every token that came before.
V is the Value: the actual information.
Notice that little word wrapped around Q and K transpose?
`softmax`.
That single function is why your prompts degrade over time.
Let's zoom into the softmax denominator on the next slide.
